MBBS in India vs MBBS Abroad: Which Option is Better?

Today, many students compare MBBS in India and MBBS Abroad.

Both programmes have their own advantages and challenges.

MBBS in India

Advantages:

  • Familiar healthcare system
  • Strong clinical exposure
  • Recognized curriculum
  • Better local networking

Challenges:

  • High competition
  • Limited government seats

MBBS Abroad

Advantages:

  • Wider seat availability
  • International exposure
  • Global learning environment

Challenges:

  • Licensing requirements
  • Country-specific regulations
  • Cultural adaptation

Therefore, students should make decisions according to their personal goals and budget.
