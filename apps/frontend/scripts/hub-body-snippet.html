A Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) is one of the most preferable and prestigious courses in India. The MBBS in India has a duration of 5.5 years, with its emphasis on academics and practical experience, and produces highly skilled physicians ready to serve patients.

India has over 600 medical colleges, 209 of which are government-run and the rest private. Every year, millions of students dream of studying MBBS in india however, few students have achieved their dreams successfully. During the MBBS course, students get theoretical as well as practical training that helps them learn how to diagnose and treat patients. 

India offers a well-respected and competitive path to becoming a medical doctor through the MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) degree. After completing the MBBS in India, students can be placed in any government or private hospital. 

Admission to MBBS programs is primarily based on NEET (National Eligibility Cum Entrance test), which is an essential entrance exam for all medical colleges including the government, private and employed universities. There are more than 700 medical colleges in India, with more than 1 Lakh MBBS seats available. The tax structure varies quite differently – government colleagues provide cheap education, while private institutions may require much higher fees.

Top board medical colleges include AIIMS, zippers and MAMC, known for its quality training and infrastructure. After completing MBBS, new graduates can choose for post -graduate expertise (MD/MS), diploma or super -speciality course or clinical practice.

Demand for MBBS in India is increasing the growing health services, making it a rewarding field for interested doctors yet.
