  • Select an NMC-approved medical university in Russia.
  • Make sure the university offers English-medium MBBS programs.

    • Submit Application

    1. Fill out the online application form.
    2. Submit required documents::
      • 10th & 12th mark sheets
      • NEET scorecard
      • Passport copy
      • Passport-size photographs

    Receive Admission Letter

    Once your documents are verified, the university will issue a provisional admission letter.

    Invitation Letter & Visa Process

    • The university will apply for an invitation letter from the Russian Ministry of Education.
    • With this letter, you can apply for a student visa at the Russian Embassy or Consulate in India.

    Pay Initial Fees

    Some universities may ask for an advance payment of tuition or administrative charges.

    Travel & Join Classes

    • Once the visa is approved, students can book their flight to Russia.
    • The university will provide airport pickup and hostel accommodation.
