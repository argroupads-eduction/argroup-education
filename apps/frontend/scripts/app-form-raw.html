Admission Process for Yaroslav-the-Wise Novgorod State Medical University

Application Submission:

  • Fill out the online application form available on the university’s official website or through authorized admission consultants.
  • Submit the necessary documents, including your passport, academic transcripts, and other relevant certificates.

Receive Admission Letter:

  • Once your application is reviewed and accepted, you will receive an Admission Letter or Offer Letter from the university, typically within a few weeks.

Document Verification:

  • Submit verified copies of all required documents, including your academic records (12th-grade mark sheets), passport copies, and health certificates.
  • Some documents may need to be notarized or legalized as per the university’s requirements.

Pay Tuition Fees:
