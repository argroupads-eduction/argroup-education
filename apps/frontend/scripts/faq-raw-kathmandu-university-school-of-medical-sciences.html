FAQs:

What is Kathmandu University School of Medical Sciences (KUSMS)?

Kathmandu University School of Medical Sciences (KUSMS) is a renowned medical institution located in Dhulikhel, Nepal. It is affiliated with Kathmandu University and offers various undergraduate and postgraduate medical programs.

What programs are offered at Kathmandu University School of Medical Sciences?

KUSMS offers a wide range of programs in the field of medical and health sciences. These include MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery), MD/MS programs, Bachelor of Nursing (BN), and various other health-related courses.

Is Kathmandu University School of Medical Sciences recognized nationally and internationally?

Yes, KUSMS is recognized by the Nepal Medical Council (NMC) and other relevant authorities in Nepal. It also holds international recognition, and its degrees are accepted worldwide, making it a preferred choice for medical education.

What is the campus and facilities like at Kathmandu University School of Medical Sciences?

The KUSMS campus is situated in a picturesque location in Dhulikhel, offering a serene and conducive environment for learning. The campus is equipped with modern facilities, including well-equipped labs, libraries, student accommodations, and teaching hospitals for practical training.
