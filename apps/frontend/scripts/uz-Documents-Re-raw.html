Documents Required for Student Visa

When applying for MBBS in Uzbekistan, Indian students need to submit the following documents: 

  • Valid Passport (minimum 2 years validity)
  • Visa Application Form
  • Passport-sized Photographs
  • Invitation Letter from Medical University
  • Original 10th & 12th Mark Sheets
  • NEET Scorecard (mandatory for Indian students)
  • HIV Test Report (issued within 3 months)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate
  • Payment Receipt for Visa Fee
